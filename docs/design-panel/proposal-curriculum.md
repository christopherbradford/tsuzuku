## 1. Fluency model and pedagogical spine (brief; this doc leads with curriculum + data)

**What produces fluency** (and what the app does about it):

| Ingredient | Mechanism in Kotodama Dojo |
|---|---|
| Thousands of hours of comprehensible input at i+1 | Every chapter ships a graded dialogue and a serialized story ("the Saga") validated to ≥95% known tokens; furigana fades per kanji mastery; TTS at 0.6–1.3× |
| Retrieval practice with spacing | FSRS-5 on every learnable item, per *facet* (recognition, production, listening, reading, writing) |
| Output under mild pressure | Typing with live romaji→kana conversion, cloze, transformation drills generated by a conjugation engine, shadowing + role-play with SpeechRecognition where available |
| Interleaving & testing effect | Review queue mixes item types and facets; chapter "Boss" and belt "Grading" are interleaved tests, not re-presentations |
| Pattern-first grammar | Grammar point = pattern template + ≤600-char plain-English explanation + ≥6 validated example sentences + contrasts/pitfalls |
| Kanji via components | Canonical 250-component list with fixed names so every mnemonic across every writer uses the same vocabulary; readings taught only through real words |
| Pitch accent awareness | Accent nucleus stored for every N5–N4 word, contour drawn over kana, "Pitch Pulse" minimal-pair drills |
| Immersion outside the app | "Field Training" log: learners log minutes of real-world listening/reading for capped XP; the app is honest that it supplies ~500 structured hours of a ~2,000-hour journey |

**Honest timeline:** 430 chapters at the default "Steady" pace (1 chapter/day, ~30 min) ≈ 14 months of new material, ~20 months at realistic 5-days/week adherence, plus a review tail. Belt 9 completion ≈ CEFR C1 in reading/listening with strong conversational fluency *if* Field Training hours are also logged. The onboarding screen says this plainly.

### 2.4 Chapter anatomy ("Chapter Kata")

Every chapter is three **Scenes** (~8–10 min each) plus a Boss. A learner can stop after any scene.

1. **Scene 1 — Forge (new items):** vocab in mini-batches of 5 → immediate quiz (1-minute learning step) → next batch → batch of 10 re-quiz. New kanji taught by components + mnemonic, immediately linked to the chapter's words.
2. **Scene 2 — Kata (grammar + sentences):** pattern card, explanation, 6–10 example sentences with audio, then cloze/transform/scramble drills on fresh sentences.
3. **Scene 3 — Field (input + output):** dialogue listen → role-play (speak or type role B) → Saga episode (i+1 reading, tap-to-gloss, 3 comprehension questions) → shadow 3 sentences.
4. **Boss:** 12–20 interleaved questions drawn from this chapter + the 3 previous, HP-bar presentation (Section 6.14).

Density rules enforced by validator: vocab 12–35, kanji 0–12, grammar 1–4, sentences ≥ 1.5 × vocab, exactly 1 dialogue, exactly 1 story, boss pool ≥ 40 eligible items.

### 2.5 The Saga (serialized graded reader)

The 425 stories form one continuing original story: the learner (name chosen at onboarding) arrives in 雲見町 (Kumomi, "cloud-view town"), a fictional mountain town, to train at a run-down dojo. Original cast fixed in the **Saga Bible** (`content/_meta/saga-bible.md`): 風間ゆき (Kazama Yuki, the retired champion who runs the dojo), 火野れん (Hino Ren, the rival), 小川みお (Ogawa Mio, the friend), 田中のおばあさん (the shopkeeper), 大将 (Taishō, the stray cat who is clearly the real master). Each arc = one story arc with a cliffhanger every 10th chapter. Episodes are written strictly in-level (ch 011 episode: ~80 chars; ch 370: ~1,200 chars). The validator checks every episode against the known-set snapshot for its chapter. "Read the next episode of your own anime" is the single strongest daily-return hook and it is 100% pedagogical.

## 3. Learnable item types and how the SRS treats each

### 3.1 Scheduler

- **Algorithm:** FSRS-5 (19 default weights shipped; per-learner optimization is out of scope for v1), desired retention 0.90 (settings: 0.85–0.95), max interval 365 d, fuzz ±5%, load balancing (pick due day in the fuzz window with the fewest scheduled reviews).
- **Card = (itemId, facet).** Cards share item data; each card has its own FSRS state.
- **Learning steps** (in-session, before FSRS takes over): kana 1m/5m/20m; vocab/kanji/grammar 1m/10m; sentences 10m. Relearning 10m.
- **Auto-rating from exercise results** (no 4-button UI except in self-graded speaking):
  - Correct, no hint, response time < 1.5× the card's median → **Good**; if card stability ≥ 30 d and time < 0.75× median → **Easy**
  - Correct with hint (tap-to-gloss, furigana reveal, second attempt after typo warning) or slow → **Hard**
  - Wrong → **Again**
- **Daily new items:** default 15 (vocab+kanji+grammar count; sentences and kana facets don't). Range 5–40. Review cap 200/day; overflow carried, ordered by lowest retrievability.
- **Mastery tiers (menkyo licenses)** by FSRS stability S: 入門 Nyūmon (<1 d) → 初伝 Shoden (1–7 d) → 中伝 Chūden (7–30 d) → 奥伝 Okuden (30–120 d) → 皆伝 Kaiden (>120 d). Kaiden cards move to a "Kaiden check" lane capped at 10/day; they never disappear, they just stop crowding the queue.
- **Nemesis (leech):** lapses ≥ 6 → the item becomes a Nemesis, is removed from the normal queue, and appears in a dedicated "Duel" screen with a rewritten mnemonic, its lookalikes side-by-side, and 3 fresh sentences. Win 3 duels on 3 distinct days → defeated → returns to the queue with stability reset to 3 d.
- **Interleaving:** due cards sorted by retrievability ascending, shuffled within windows of 10, constraint: no two consecutive cards share an item, and facet types alternate where possible.
- **Return-from-absence ("Back from the Mountain"):** if ≥ 7 days inactive, the backlog is spread evenly over the next 7 days. No shame screen.

### 3.2 Facets per item type

| Item | Facets (card keys) | Introduced | Gated unlock | Retires? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Kana** | `recog` (kana→romaji), `prod` (sound/romaji→type kana) | both at intro | — | Yes: at S > 180 d, card is archived (kana will be exercised by everything else) |
| **Vocab** | `mean` (JP→EN), `read` (kanji word→kana), `listen` (audio→type), `prod` (EN→type JP) | `mean` + `read` at intro | `listen` when `mean` S ≥ 1 d; `prod` when `mean` S ≥ 3 d | No (Kaiden lane) |
| **Kanji** | `mean` (char→meaning via components), `read` (char→on/kun through 2 words), `write` (stroke-order canvas, optional) | `mean` at intro | `read` after S ≥ 1 d; `write` only if learner enabled writing | No |
| **Grammar** | `cloze`, `transform` (engine-generated), `prod` (EN→type full JP sentence) | `cloze` at intro | `transform` after 2 d; `prod` after 7 d | No |
| **Sentence** | `dict` (dictation), `shadow` (speak) | `dict` at intro; `shadow` offered in Scene 3 | — | Yes: at S > 60 d sentence cards graduate (words/grammar keep carrying the load) |
| **Pitch** (facet of vocab, N5–N4 only) | `pitch` (choose contour) | after `mean` S ≥ 7 d | learner can disable | Yes: S > 90 d |

Card key format: `${itemId}#${facet}`, e.g. `v:n5:02:017#prod`.

## 4. Content Data Model (TypeScript schemas)

All schemas live in `src/content/schema.ts` as zod schemas with inferred types; the build-time validator and the runtime loader import the same file. `schemaVersion` is bumped only on breaking changes.

### 4.1 Common

```ts
export type Level = 'kana' | 'n5' | 'n4' | 'n3' | 'n2' | 'n1' | 'bx'; // bx = beyond
export type Register =
  | 'neutral' | 'polite' | 'casual' | 'formal' | 'written'
  | 'sonkeigo' | 'kenjougo' | 'slang' | 'dialect';
export type Dialect = 'kansai' | 'tohoku' | 'hakata' | 'hiroshima' | 'okinawa';

export interface FileHeader {
  schema: 'kana' | 'vocab' | 'kanji' | 'grammar' | 'sentences' | 'dialogues' | 'stories' | 'chapters';
  schemaVersion: 1;
  level: Level;
  arc: number;           // 0..43, the writer's assignment unit
  part?: number;         // 1..n when a type needs >1 file per arc (sentences)
  writer: string;        // free tag, e.g. "claude-2026-10-arc02"
  generatedAt: string;   // ISO 8601
}

export interface ContentFile<T> extends FileHeader { items: T[] }

export interface MCQ {
  q: string;               // question in English (N5–N4) or Japanese (N3+), ≤ 120 chars
  choices: [string, string, string, string];
  answer: 0 | 1 | 2 | 3;
  explain?: string;        // ≤ 160 chars
}
```

```ts

export interface VocabItem {

  pitch: number[];         // accent nucleus per mora, 0 = heiban; required for n5/n4
  pitchVerified?: boolean; // default false; set true by human review
  kanji: string[];         // every kanji in `surface` as ids: ['k:食']
  transitivity?: 'vt' | 'vi';
  pair?: string;           // transitive/intransitive partner id
  usuallyKana?: boolean;
  keigoOf?: string;        // 召し上がる → v:...(食べる)
  variantOf?: string;      // dialect variant of a standard word
  dialect?: Dialect;
  register?: Register;     // default 'neutral'
  tags: string[];          // ≤ 6 from the controlled tag list (food, time, body, school, …)
  note?: string;           // ≤ 200 chars usage note (nuance, collocation, false friend)
  examples: string[];      // ≥ 2 sentence ids in this chapter or earlier
  chapter: string;         // ch:023
  freq?: number;           // corpus rank if known
}
```

```ts
  level: Level;
  chapter: string;
  pattern: string;         // 'V-て + ください'   (template using V/N/Adj-い/Adj-な/S)

}
```

### 4.6 Sentences

```ts
export interface Sentence {
  id: string;              // s:n5:03:0172
  jp: string;              // 私は毎朝コーヒーを飲みます。
  furigana: string;        // {私|わたし}は{毎朝|まいあさ}コーヒーを{飲|の}みます。
  kana: string;            // わたしはまいあさコーヒーをのみます。  (TTS text; kana only)
  en: string;              // natural English
  altEn?: string[];        // ≤ 4 extra accepted translations
  altJp?: string[];        // ≤ 4 accepted production variants (kana-only form is auto-accepted)
  targets: { vocab: string[]; grammar: string[]; kanji: string[] };  // what this sentence exercises
  cloze?: { answer: string; distractors?: string[] }[];  // answer must occur exactly once in jp
  register: Register;
  dialect?: Dialect;
  pitch?: string;          // optional mora contour 'LHHHLL…' (length = mora count of `kana`)
  chapter: string;
  tags?: string[];
}
```

Furigana markup grammar: `text := (plain | ruby)*; ruby := '{' base '|' reading '}'`; `base` contains ≥ 1 kanji and no braces; `reading` is hiragana/katakana only. Concatenating all `base`+`plain` must equal `jp`. Okurigana stays outside the braces (`{飲|の}みます`).

Chunking rule: vocab and sentence files **100–200 items**; dialogue and story files 5–20; kanji/grammar files 10–60; chapter files exactly 10. One arc = one writer packet = one directory worth of files. The app lazy-loads a belt's files on entry and caches them in memory; `?v=<sha256[:8]>` cache-busts.

### 5.2 ID grammar (regex-enforced)

| Type | Pattern | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Kana | `^kana:(hi\|ka):[a-z]{1,4}$` | `kana:hi:kya` |
| Vocab | `^v:(n5\|n4\|n3\|n2\|n1\|bx):\d{2}:\d{3}$` | `v:n5:02:017` |
| Kanji | `^k:[\u3400-\u9FFF]$` | `k:食` |
| Grammar | `^g:(n5\|…\|bx):\d{2}:\d{3}$` (+ unique `slug`) | `g:n5:03:004` |
| Sentence | `^s:(n5\|…\|bx):\d{2}:\d{4}$` | `s:n5:03:0172` |
| Dialogue | `^d:(n5\|…\|bx):\d{2}:\d{2}$` | `d:n5:03:04` |

The middle `\d{2}` is the **arc number**, which doubles as the writer's namespace: two writers can never collide because each owns one arc. IDs are immutable once shipped; renames go through `manifest.redirects`.

## 6. Exercise Types — exact interaction specs

Common rules: Japanese input fields run a built-in romaji→kana converter (wanakana-style table; `nn`→ん, `xtsu`/`ltsu`→っ, `-`→ー, Shift for katakana or auto-katakana for katakana-tagged answers). Answer normalization: NFKC, strip whitespace/punctuation, full-width↔half-width digits, accept kana-only spelling of any kanji answer via the item's `reading`/`kana`. Every exercise emits `{cardKey, correct, hintUsed, ms}` → rating.

| # | Exercise | Shown | Learner does | Correct when | Ki |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Kana Flash** (`recog`) | one kana, 1.5 s auto-audio | type romaji; first 2 exposures show 4 choices instead | Hepburn match (alt spellings `si/shi`, `tu/tsu`, `hu/fu` accepted) | 2 |
| 2 | **Kana Forge** (`prod`) | audio or romaji | type the kana (converter live) | exact kana | 2 |
| 3 | **Meaning Strike** (vocab `mean`) | surface (+furigana per policy), audio button | type English; or 4-choice during first 2 exposures | matches any `meanings[]` with Levenshtein ≤ 1 for ≤ 5 chars, ≤ 2 otherwise; article/“to ” stripped | 3 |
| 4 | **Reading Strike** (vocab `read`, kanji `read`) | kanji word, no furigana | type kana reading | exact `reading` | 3 |
| 5 | **Echo** (vocab/sentence `listen`/`dict`) | audio only at learner's speed (0.6–1.3); replay ≤ 3 (3rd replay = hint) | type what you heard | matches `jp`, `kana`, or `altJp` after normalization | 5 |
| 6 | **Production Dash** (vocab/grammar `prod`) | English + register tag | type Japanese | matches `surface`/`reading` (vocab) or `jp`/`kana`/`altJp` (sentence) | 6 |

## 7. Content Production Pipeline (dozens of AI writers in parallel)

### 7.1 Phases

1. **Phase A — Architect (sequential, once):** generate the three canonical lists (`wordlist.csv` 11,600 rows, `kanjilist.csv` 2,336 rows, `grammarlist.csv` 680 rows), each row pre-assigned to a chapter; `components.json`, `tags.json`, `denylist.json`, `saga-bible.md`. Because every word/kanji/grammar point's *introduction chapter is fixed before writing*, the known-set for any chapter is computable without waiting for other writers.
2. **Phase B — Writers (44 parallel packets, one per arc):** `npm run packet -- --arc 3` emits a prompt bundle: schemas, style guide, the arc's slices of the canonical lists, the known-set snapshot (all surfaces/readings introduced before the arc, JSON), the Saga bible + previous arc synopsis, three gold example items per type, and the exact file names to produce. The writer returns the 7 files for the arc.
3. **Phase C — Validate & repair loop:** `npm run validate -- --arc 3` → `report.json` with `{file, id, rule, message, fix?}` entries. Errors are fed back to the same writer with the instruction "fix only the listed items". Loop until zero errors; warnings reviewed by a human.
4. **Phase D — Merge & build:** `npm run content:build` recomputes `charCount`, sorts items by id, runs prettier, computes sha256, writes `manifest.json`, bumps `contentVersion`.
5. **Phase E — Linkers (small, parallel):** cross-arc passes that add `lookalikes`, `contrasts`, and `pair` references, which require seeing neighboring arcs.

### 7.2 Validation rules (build-time, Node; zod + kuromoji tokenizer)

**Structural**
- V01 File parses; header matches path (`schema`, `level`, `arc`, `part`); `schemaVersion` supported.
- V02 Every item validates against its zod schema; unknown keys fail.
- V03 Chunk size within band for its schema (vocab/sentences 100–200, etc.).
- V04 Ids match the regex for their type; arc segment equals header `arc`; global uniqueness across the whole tree; grammar `slug` unique.
- V05 Every referenced id resolves (`examples`, `vocab`, `kanji`, `targets.*`, `pair`, `keigoOf`, `variantOf`, `contrasts.withId`, chapter fields, `newVocab/newKanji/newGrammar`, `dialogue`, `story`).
- V06 Canonical coverage: every row in `wordlist.csv`/`kanjilist.csv`/`grammarlist.csv` appears exactly once in content, in its assigned chapter, with matching surface/reading/slug.

**Linguistic**
- V10 `reading`/`kana` contain only kana + ー (plus 、。！？ and spaces in sentences).
- V11 Every kanji character in `surface` has an id in `kanji[]` and vice versa; each listed kanji is introduced in this chapter or earlier (hard fail) unless `usuallyKana` with kana `surface`.
- V12 Furigana parses; bases + plain text concatenate to `jp`; every kanji run is inside a ruby; readings in rubies concatenate (with okurigana) to `kana`.
- V13 **i+1 constraint:** tokenize `jp` with kuromoji; each content-word lemma must be in known-set(chapter) ∪ chapter's new items ∪ allowlist (saga character names, numerals, place names from the bible, interjections). Sentences: ≤ 1 unknown lemma → fail at 2. Dialogues: ≤ 2 unknown per dialogue. Stories: unknown lemmas ⊆ `plusOne[]` (≤ 3) and known-token ratio ≥ 0.95.
- V14 Grammar coverage: every `GrammarPoint` has ≥ 6 example sentences whose `targets.grammar` includes it, and each example contains at least one `clozeHints` substring.
- V15 Every vocab has ≥ 2 example sentences; every kanji ≥ 2 vocab; every sentence has ≥ 1 target.
- V16 `pitch[]` values in `[0, moraCount(reading)]`; required for n5/n4.
- V17 `cloze[].answer` occurs exactly once in `jp`.
- V18 Sentence length bands (chars): n5 ≤ 18, n4 ≤ 28, n3 ≤ 40, n2 ≤ 55, n1 ≤ 75, bx ≤ 75. Story length bands per Section 4.8.
- V19 Component names in `KanjiItem.components[].name` must exist in `components.json`; the mnemonic must contain each component name (case-insensitive) — this is what keeps 44 writers' mnemonics mutually consistent.
- V20 Register consistency: a sentence tagged `polite` must end in ます/です forms (regex heuristic, warning level); `casual` must not.

**Safety & policy**
- V30 Denylist hit anywhere (franchise/character/brand names) → fail.
- V31 No real living persons' names; no romaji inside `jp`/`kana`/`furigana` except Latin-script loanwords flagged with tag `latin-ok`.
- V32 Duplicate `surface`+`reading` across files → fail unless both carry `tags: ['homograph']` with distinct meanings.

**Chapter-level**
- V40 Density bands (Section 2.4); boss pool size; `estMinutes` 20–45; exactly one dialogue and one story; story `episode` = chapter index when `kind: 'saga'`.

### 7.3 Writer style guide (the part writers read most)

- Natural Japanese first; textbook stiffness allowed only in Arcs 2–3. From Arc 4 on, dialogues must include at least one あいづち and one sentence-final particle.
- Glosses: lowercase, no trailing period, verbs as "to eat", nouns bare, adjectives bare. ≤ 3 glosses, most frequent first.
- Explanations: start with the pattern and one canonical example, then *when to use it*, then *what it's not*. Address the learner as "you". No IPA, no Latin grammar terms beyond noun/verb/adjective/particle.
- Mnemonics: concrete image, one sentence, uses canonical component names, never a copyrighted character.
- Saga: present tense feel, short paragraphs, every episode ends on a hook or a warm beat; recurring cast only; new named character requires a bible entry.
- Cultural notes ≤ 120 chars, factual, no stereotypes.
- Pitch: give your best standard-Tokyo accent; leave `pitchVerified` false.

## 8. Progression Systems

### 8.1 Ki (XP) economy

| Action | Ki |
|---|---|
| Review correct / Again (effort counts) | 3 / 1 |
| New item first-pass completed | 5 |
| Cloze / Form Forge / Scramble | 4 |
| Echo / Production | 5 / 6 |
| Shadow (SR / self-graded) | 6 / 4 |
| Pitch Pulse | 3 |
| Scene complete / Chapter complete | 15 / 60 |
| Boss win / flawless bonus | 100 / +50 |
| Belt Grading pass | 500 |
| Role-play / Saga Read | 25 / 30 (+5 per correct MCQ) |
| Nemesis defeated | 20 |
| Field Training | 1 per minute, cap 100/day |
| Speedrun | 1 per correct, cap 60/day |

**Daily goal tiers:** Spark 50 (keeps the Flame), Train 150 (default target), Kiai 300. A typical Steady day (10 min reviews + 1 chapter) yields ~220 Ki.

### 8.2 Power Level (1–99)

Ki to go from level L to L+1 = `100 + 25·L`. Cumulative to 99 ≈ 131,000 Ki ≈ 600 Steady days. Title bands: 1–9 入門者 Nyūmonsha · 10–24 門下生 Monkasei · 25–39 師範代 Shihandai · 40–59 師範 Shihan · 60–79 達人 Tatsujin · 80–98 宗師 Sōshi · 99 言霊使い Kotodama-tsukai. Level is cosmetic + unlocks avatar gi trims; it never gates content.

### 8.3 Belt promotion (Grading)

Eligibility: all bosses in the belt beaten; ≥ 80% of the belt's vocab and grammar at Chūden or above (S ≥ 7 d); ≥ 70% of the belt's kanji at Chūden+. Then the Grading (40 Q, ≥ 85%). Failing shows exactly which items to train; retry allowed as soon as due reviews are cleared. No hearts, no cooldown timers.

### 8.4 Pace presets

| Pace | New items/day | Chapters | Est. daily minutes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Casual 気楽 | 8 | ½ chapter (1–2 scenes) | 15 |
| Steady 一歩一歩 (default) | 15 | 1 | 30 |
| Shugyō 修行 | 30 | 2 | 60; app shows projected review load and asks for confirmation |

### 8.5 The Flame (streak) with forgiveness

- A day counts at ≥ 50 Ki or clearing all due reviews (whichever is easier).
- **Rest Scrolls (freeze tokens):** earn 1 per 7-day streak, bank up to 3, auto-applied on a missed day (toggleable). Using one is *celebrated* ("Rest is training").
- **Weekly Flame:** a parallel counter of weeks with ≥ 5 active days. A single missed day never zeroes this.
- **Rekindle:** after a break, 3 consecutive active days restores the previous streak count minus the days missed (floored at 50% of the old streak). Framed as the Phoenix badge, not a loss.
- **Vacation mode:** up to 30 days/year; freezes the Flame; backlog spreads on return.
- Streak status never removes badges, wallpapers, levels or belts. Reminders: one optional local notification per day, off by default.

### 8.6 Anti-dark-pattern commitments (shipped in About page)

No loot boxes, no randomized rewards, no timed "limited" wallpapers, no purchases, no leaderboards against strangers, no guilt copy. Every reward has a deterministic, visible condition. All streak math is explained in-app.

## 11. Learner State (localStorage) and backup

```ts
export interface LearnerState {
  v: 1;
  profile: { name: string; nameKatakana: string; spiritKanji: string; startedAt: string;
             pace: 'casual'|'steady'|'shugyo'; retention: number; furiganaPolicy: 'auto'|'always'|'never';
             ttsVoice?: string; ttsRate: number; writingEnabled: boolean; pitchEnabled: boolean; reminders: boolean };
  srs: Record<string, [due: number, s: number, d: number, reps: number, lapses: number, last: number, state: 0|1|2|3]>;
  // due/last in minutes since epoch; ~40 bytes per card; 45k cards ≈ 1.8 MB
  progress: { chapter: number; scene: 0|1|2|3; bossesWon: number[]; bossesFlawless: number[]; gradings: Record<number, { passedAt: string; score: number }>; nemeses: Record<string, { duelsWon: number; defeatedAt?: string }> };
  ki: { total: number; level: number; days: Record<string /* YYYY-MM-DD */, number> };
  flame: { current: number; best: number; weekly: number; scrolls: number; lastActive: string; vacationUntil?: string };
  stats: Record<string, number>;   // reviews, echoFast, shadowGood, storiesRead, fieldMinutes, bestCombo, …
  badges: Record<string, string>;  // id → ISO unlock date
  wallpapers: Record<string, string>;
  favoritePhrase?: string;
  contentVersion: string;
}
```

Storage: single key `kotodama.v1` written on a 2-second debounce + on `visibilitychange`. Export = `Blob` download `kotodama-backup-YYYY-MM-DD.json`; import = file input with schema validation and a merge-or-replace choice. A gentle "it's been 7 days since your last backup" line appears on the home screen (dismissable, never modal). If localStorage approaches 4.5 MB, Kaiden kana and graduated sentence cards are compacted first.

## 12. Architecture notes (Vite + Preact + signals, hash routing)

```
src/
  main.tsx            router (#/dojo, #/train, #/chapter/:id, #/review, #/arcade, #/saga, #/kanji, #/grammar,
                      #/gallery, #/badges, #/stats, #/settings, #/backup, #/about)
  content/            schema.ts (zod), loader.ts (manifest + lazy chunk fetch via import.meta.env.BASE_URL), known-set.ts
  srs/                fsrs.ts, queue.ts (interleaving, caps, lanes), rating.ts
  engine/             kana.ts (romaji→kana), conjugate.ts (POS × ConjForm), normalize.ts, similarity.ts, pitch.ts
  audio/              tts.ts (voice pick, rate), stt.ts (SpeechRecognition feature-detect, fallbacks)
  exercises/          one component per exercise type, shared <AnswerInput/> and <ResultBanner/>
  rewards/            badges.ts (rule evaluator on state change), wallpapers/*.ts (renderers), png.ts
  state/              store.ts (signals), persist.ts, export.ts
  ui/                 LCARS-free, retro-arcade design tokens; fonts: Noto Sans JP + DotGothic16 (Google Fonts, OFL)
scripts/
  packet.ts  validate.ts  build-manifest.ts  known-set.ts
tests/ (Vitest)
  schema.test.ts, fsrs.test.ts (golden intervals), conjugate.test.ts (golden table of 60 verbs × 20 forms),
  kana.test.ts, normalize.test.ts, queue.test.ts, badges.test.ts, validate.fixtures.test.ts,
  content.test.ts (runs the validator over shipped content in CI)
```

GitHub Pages: `base: '/<repo>/'` in `vite.config.ts`; hash routing avoids 404s; `public/content` is served as static files; a `404.html` copy of `index.html` is unnecessary with hash routes. CI: `npm test && npm run content:validate && npm run build`, then deploy `dist/` via `actions/deploy-pages`.

## 13. What ships in v1, and how the tree grows

**v1 (launch):**

|---|---|---|---|

| v1.2 | Belts 5–6 (N3) | 091–170 | 8 |

Rules that make growth painless: IDs immutable; chapter indices global and pre-allocated to 430; the manifest is the only file the app reads unconditionally; a newer `contentVersion` only ever *adds* files or *redirects* ids; learner state never references file paths, only ids; the validator runs in CI on every content PR so a bad chunk cannot deploy.

**Human QA budget per release:** 1 native-speaker review pass on dialogues and Saga episodes (highest-visibility text), 1 spot check of 10% of sentences, full automated validation on everything else.
